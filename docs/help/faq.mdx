## Do I need Daylight Calendar Card?

No. **Daylight Calendar Import** adds events to Home Assistant calendars after your approval. **[Daylight Calendar Card](https://docs.daylightcalendar.com)** is a separate dashboard card that displays events. They work together, but neither requires the other.

## Do I need a Daylight Cloud account or subscription?

No. The self-hosted integration uses your own Home Assistant **AI Task** entity. Your chosen AI provider may charge for requests or retain submitted content according to its own policies. See [Privacy and retention](/help/privacy).

## Will Daylight put events on my calendar automatically?

No. Text, files, and optional email intake create drafts for you to review. **Only explicit approval writes a calendar event.** You can edit or reject drafts beforehand. See [Review and approve](/workflows/review).

## Can I import a scanned PDF or photo?

Only when your selected AI Task provider and model support that attachment type. Text-layer PDFs can be read locally and sent to a text-only provider. See [Text, images, and PDFs](/workflows/manual-import).

## Can I import multiple events from one announcement?

Yes. Daylight can produce multiple drafts from one message or file, and each draft must be reviewed. General repeating schedules are different: Daylight does not create recurring RRULE events from natural language.

## Do I have to connect an email inbox?

No. Start with **New import** and paste text or upload a supported image/PDF. IMAP email intake is optional and should use a dedicated folder or account. See [Optional email intake](/configuration/email).

## What happens if the AI makes a mistake?

Review and edit the title, date, time, destination calendar, and other fields. Check for duplicates and overlaps. If a calendar write is reported as uncertain, inspect the destination calendar before resolving it—don't blindly retry. See [Review and approve](/workflows/review).

## Can I use any Home Assistant calendar?

You need at least one calendar that supports creating events. Daylight can also observe separate read-only calendars for scheduling conflicts; observation alone does not grant permission to write to them.

## Where do I get help?

Start with [Troubleshooting](/help/troubleshooting) and the [Advanced reference](/help/advanced-reference). You can also [report a reproducible issue](https://github.com/superdingo101/daylight-calendar-import/issues), using fictional sample events and sanitized logs.
