# v0.9.0 — public beta

**Status:** v0.9.0 is a release candidate, not yet approved for publication.
The [v0.9.0-beta0 prerelease](https://github.com/superdingo101/daylight-calendar-import/releases/tag/v0.9.0-beta0)
is available for testing. Final v0.9.0 still requires the release checks
described below.

## Highlights

- **Import from anywhere in Daylight Imports.** The header's **New import**
  action opens a dedicated screen for pasting event details or uploading an
  image/PDF, without cluttering the Review inbox. It is available from Review
  inbox, Recent activity, Settings and import details. A compact refresh icon
  and consistent **Daylight Imports** title simplify navigation.
- **More useful conflict review.** Before approval confirmation, Daylight
  checks the configured conflict-observation calendars and shows existing
  event times and overlap intervals alongside possible conflicts. These
  observations are advisory; incomplete checks are reported rather than
  presented as a clear calendar.
- **Safer handling of recurring announcements.** Known recurring schedules
  are flagged instead of silently becoming ordinary one-off drafts. A
  recurrence warning blocks approval, including after restart or reprocessing,
  so the schedule can be handled manually or as explicit separate dates.
  This is a safeguard, **not** recurring-event creation.
- **Clearer notification failures.** Recent activity surfaces notifications
  that permanently failed delivery, with guidance to review notification
  settings. This does not change the outcome of an import or calendar write.
- **Extraction and input safeguards.** Source text and attachments are
  explicitly treated as untrusted evidence in AI Task extraction prompts.
  Source text is limited to 1,000,000 Unicode characters, and new or
  replacement routing-alias sets are limited to 100 entries. These are
  defense-in-depth measures, not a guarantee of AI accuracy or prompt safety.

## Existing workflows included in this beta

Paste an announcement or upload a JPEG, PNG, WebP or PDF; optional **Direct
IMAP** can also ingest forwarded emails and supported attachments. A source
may produce several editable event drafts. Review or reject drafts
individually or in bulk, choose an allowed destination calendar, and approve
before anything is written to that calendar. Default calendars, routing hints,
duplicate checks, pending sensors and opt-in notifications remain available.
No Daylight Cloud account or subscription is needed.

The beta-readiness work also expands automated checks for installation and
historical upgrades, stored drafts and settings, permissions, desktop/mobile
navigation, routing and conflict review, approval and uncertain-write
recovery, IMAP, AI Task dispatch, privacy, large inputs and concurrent
approvals. These tests do **not** establish live-model extraction accuracy
or replace testing on a real Home Assistant installation.

## Installation and upgrade

Home Assistant **2026.7.4 or newer**, a configured **AI Task** entity and at
least one writable allowed/default calendar are required. For images and
scanned PDFs, the chosen AI Task provider/model must support the needed
attachments; PDFs with usable text layers can work with a text-only provider.

When upgrading from v0.8.0:

1. **Back up Home Assistant**, including the integration's private pending
   import storage and configuration.
2. Update Daylight Calendar Import through HACS and **restart Home Assistant**.
   Keep the existing integration entry, AI Task configuration, calendars and
   stored review work in place.
3. Open **Daylight Imports**, verify settings and pending drafts, and review
   imported details before approving any events.

**Do not remove and re-add the integration to perform an upgrade or repair.**
Removal deletes local pending work, lifecycle history and deduplication
fingerprints, but leaves previously created calendar events in place.
For an uncertain calendar write, inspect the destination calendar before
selecting its explicit recovery outcome; do not blindly retry or resend the
source.

See [manual imports](../manual-import.md) for the no-email workflow and the
[public beta checklist](../public-beta-checklist.md) for installation and
upgrade verification.

## Known limitations

- AI providers can miss or misinterpret dates, time zones, locations, event
  counts or meeting details. Check every draft before approval. Recorded
  provider-response tests are not live provider/model certification.
- Visual image and scanned-PDF extraction depends on the selected provider
  and model. Compatible media formats alone do not guarantee successful
  extraction.
- Conflict-calendar observations are advisory and can be incomplete;
  destination-calendar duplicate and permission protections still apply.
- Recurrence detection is best-effort, especially for uncommon or non-English
  language. Automatic RRULE/recurring-event creation and automatic changes
  or deletion of existing calendar events are not supported.
- Direct IMAP is optional, currently uses a single dedicated
  password/app-password mailbox, and is not hosted forwarding. Hosted email,
  billing and SMS/MMS are not part of this release.
- Notification delivery is at least once and may repeat after a crash.
  Uncertain calendar writes require inspection and explicit resolution;
  Daylight does not blindly replay those writes.
- CI covers the minimum supported Home Assistant version and its pinned
  2026.9 line; that does not certify every newer HA version or AI model.
